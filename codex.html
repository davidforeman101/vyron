The World of Vyron

Vyron is a civilisation on the edge of known space.

For generations, the Vyrion have defended their borders against relentless waves of hostile forces.

> Survival is not guaranteed — it is earned.

The Vyrion

The Vyrion are the people of Vyron. Every Vyrion is both citizen and defender.

They are defined not by what they destroy, but by what they choose to protect.

> You are Vyrion.

The Vyra

The Vyra is the code the Vyrion live by — an ethic of resilience, precision, and purpose. It teaches that survival without principle is merely existing.

The Vyra demands excellence: in combat, in judgement, in the protection of those who cannot fight.

Vyra — Ship AI

Your ship's AI is named Vyra — a tribute to the code that defines the Vyrion way of life. She monitors systems, tracks threats, and offers guidance.

Her personality is derived from decades of constant battles, but she shines through: a light in the darkness of the void.

> Vyra is not just a tool. She is a companion forged from the principles you defend.
