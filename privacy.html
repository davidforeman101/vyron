Privacy Policy

iPhone & macOS · Last updated 7 August 2026

VYRON does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

The game runs entirely on your device. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party code.

> Zero telemetry. We genuinely don't know who you are.

The short version

Collected
Nothing. No personal data, no usage data, no identifiers.
Sent to us
Nothing. We operate no servers for VYRON to talk to.
Tracking
None. No advertising identifier, no profiling, no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.
Third parties
None. No analytics, advertising or attribution SDKs are built into the game.
Apple services
iCloud sync, Game Center and the App Store rating prompt — all optional, all operated by Apple.
Sold or shared
Never. There is nothing to sell, and we would not sell it if there were.

Data we collect

None. VYRON contains no analytics, no tracking identifiers, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party code of any kind. Nothing about you or the way you play is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent — the game makes no network connections of its own.

The game does not ask for, and cannot access, your contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera.

Game saves

Your progress — save slots, achievements, credits and settings — is stored on your own device and encrypted at rest using Apple's standard cryptography. It never leaves your device except through iCloud sync, below.

Deleting the app removes its local data in the normal way.

iCloud sync

Optional

If you're signed in to iCloud, VYRON syncs your saves between your own devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That data lives in your private iCloud account. The developer cannot see it, cannot reach it, and receives no copy of it.

Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Turn iCloud Drive off and the game keeps working — saves simply stay on the one device.

Game Center

Optional

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable — achievements included — without it.

Game Center is operated by Apple. Anything it processes, such as your Game Center nickname and your scores, is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no personal information from it.

The rating prompt

VYRON may occasionally ask you to rate it. That prompt is presented and handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, which limits how often it can appear. We are not told whether you rated the game, or what you said.

Network access

VYRON makes no network connections of its own. The only network activity the game can cause is Apple-operated — iCloud sync, Game Center and the rating prompt — and all of it is optional. The game is fully playable with no connection at all.

If you email us

If you contact VyronAdmin@proton.me we receive your email address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply to you.

We never add you to a mailing list, never share your address, and delete correspondence once it has served its purpose.

Children

VYRON collects no data from anyone, of any age. There is no chat, no user-generated content, no social feature and no advertising, so there is nothing for a child to disclose and no way for anyone to reach them through the game.

Your rights

Privacy law gives you the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal data a company holds about you. In our case the answer is simple: we hold none, so there is nothing to request and nothing to erase.

Data held in your own iCloud account or Game Center profile is managed by you, through Apple, in your device settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about privacy in VYRON generally: VyronAdmin@proton.me
