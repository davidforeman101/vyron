Privacy Policy
iPhone & macOS · Last updated 6 August 2026

VYRON does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data.

The game works fully on-device. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

> Zero telemetry. We genuinely don't know who you are.

Data we collect

None. VYRON contains no analytics, tracking identifiers, advertising SDKs, or third-party code of any kind. Nothing about you or your play is sent to us — we operate no servers.

Game saves

Your progress is stored on your device and encrypted at rest. Saves never leave your device except via iCloud sync, below.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you're signed in to iCloud, VYRON syncs saves across your own devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. That data lives in your private iCloud account and is not accessible to the developer. Apple's handling is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center (optional)

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it. Game Center is operated by Apple, and anything it processes — such as your Game Center nickname and scores — is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no personal information from it.

Network access

VYRON makes no network connections of its own. The only network activity is Apple-operated: iCloud sync and Game Center, both optional.

If you email us

If you contact VyronAdmin@proton.me we receive your address and whatever you choose to tell us. We use it only to reply, we never add you to a mailing list, and we never share it.

Children

VYRON collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address.
