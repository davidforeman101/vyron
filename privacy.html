Privacy Policy

VYRON (iPhone & macOS) — Last updated: 30 July 2026

VYRON does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. The game is designed to work fully on-device, with no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

Data We Collect

None. VYRON contains no analytics, tracking identifiers, advertising SDKs, or any third-party code. Nothing about you or your play is sent to us — we operate no servers.

Game Saves

Your progress is stored on your device, encrypted at rest. Saves never leave your device except via iCloud sync (below).

iCloud Sync (Optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, VYRON syncs your save games across your own devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. This data lives in your private iCloud account; it is not accessible to the developer. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center (Optional)

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple's Game Center. Signing in is optional — the game is fully playable without it. Game Center is operated by Apple, and any data it processes (such as your Game Center nickname and scores) is handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer receives no personal information from Game Center.

Network Access

VYRON makes no network connections of its own. The only network activity is Apple-operated: iCloud sync and Game Center, both optional.

Children

VYRON collects no data from anyone, including children.
