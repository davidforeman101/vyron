Saves & iCloud

Save from the pause menu into any of six slots — keep a run you're proud of, and still start something new without losing it.

Signed in to iCloud, your saves follow you between iPhone and Mac. Save on one, load on the other, carry on where you stopped. Saves are encrypted on your device, so progress is earned rather than edited.

Nothing here needs an account, and none of it goes near a server of ours — the sync runs entirely through your own private iCloud. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Game Center

There are 136 achievements in VYRON — kill and score milestones, every boss, every bonus round, and four full Arcade ladders.

Sign in to Game Center and you'll appear on the leaderboard, with 49 of those achievements mirrored to your Game Center profile. Apple caps what any one game can publish there, so we surface the meaningful milestones and roll each Arcade ladder up into a single completion award.

Signing in is entirely optional. Skip it and every achievement still tracks and unlocks in game, exactly as it would otherwise.

Settings & Performance

Frame rate
iPhone. Settings → Frame Rate offers 30, 60 or AUTO. 30 runs coolest and is the default; AUTO balances smoothness against temperature on a long session.
Resolution
Mac. Settings → Resolution sets the window size; Display settings control the level of detail.
Key bindings
Mac. Every control can be remapped in Settings, and game controllers are supported out of the box.

Full controls for both platforms are in the pilot's manual.

Frequently asked

My saves don't appear on my other device.

Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive turned on, then relaunch the game — sync usually completes within a minute. If it still won't, email us and we'll work through it with you.

Is there anything to buy?

No. One purchase and you have the whole game — no in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions. There is nothing else to sell you.

Where did my progress go?

Progress lives in whichever slot you saved it to. Choose LOAD GAME and pick your most recent — there are six slots, so it's easy to save over the wrong one.

An achievement unlocked in game but not on Game Center.

That's expected for some of them. Apple limits how many achievements a game can publish, so the four Arcade ladders each appear on Game Center as one award, granted when you've cleared every round in that ladder. In-game, each round still counts individually.

Do I need to be online?

No. VYRON is entirely playable offline. Leaderboards and cross-device sync need a connection, but nothing else does.

The game runs warm on my iPhone.

Set Settings → Frame Rate to 30. It's the coolest-running option and costs you very little — the game is built around it.

I found a bug.

Please email VyronAdmin@proton.me. See Reporting a problem above for what helps most.
