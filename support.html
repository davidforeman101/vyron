VYRON — Support

100 levels of relentless space combat, for iPhone and Mac.

Controls

iPhone: touch to move — your ship fires automatically. Tap the pause button to pause; tap the bolt to unleash the Vyric Surge when charged.

Mac: move with the mouse (or keyboard). P/Esc pauses; S fires the Vyric Surge. A full control reference is on the main menu.

Saving & iCloud

Save from the pause menu. If you're signed in to iCloud, saves sync automatically between your iPhone and Mac — save on one, load on the other. Saves are encrypted; progress is earned, not edited.

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center to appear on the leaderboard and earn the 49 achievements. If you skip sign-in, everything else works normally.

Performance Tips

On iPhone, the Frame Rate setting (Settings → Frame Rate) offers 30 / 60 / AUTO. 30 runs coolest; AUTO balances smoothness and temperature during long sessions.

Frequently Asked

My saves don't appear on my other device. Check both devices are signed in to the same Apple ID with iCloud Drive enabled, then relaunch the game — sync usually completes within a minute.

Is there anything to buy? No. VYRON has no in-app purchases, no ads, and no accounts.

Where did my progress go? Progress is per-save-slot. Load your latest save from the LOAD GAME menu.
