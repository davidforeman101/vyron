Controls

iPhone: touch to move — your ship fires automatically. Tap the pause button to pause; tap the bolt to unleash the Vyric Surge when charged.

Mac: move with the mouse (or keyboard). P/Esc pauses; S fires the Vyric Surge. A full control reference is on the main menu, and keys can be remapped in Settings.

Saving & iCloud

Save from the pause menu. If you're signed in to iCloud, saves sync automatically between your iPhone and Mac — save on one, load on the other. Saves are encrypted; progress is earned, not edited.

Game Center

Sign in to Game Center to appear on the leaderboard and earn the 49 achievements. If you skip sign-in, everything else works normally.

Performance

On iPhone, Settings → Frame Rate offers 30 / 60 / AUTO. 30 runs coolest; AUTO balances smoothness and temperature during long sessions.

On Mac, Settings → Resolution sets the window size, and Display settings control detail level.

Frequently asked

My saves don't appear on my other device. Check both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive enabled, then relaunch the game — sync usually completes within a minute. If it still doesn't, email us.

Is there anything to buy? No. VYRON is a single purchase — no in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts.

Where did my progress go? Progress is per-save-slot. Load your latest save from the LOAD GAME menu.

I found a bug. Please email VyronAdmin@proton.me with your device and what happened. Bug reports genuinely shape what gets fixed first.
