Controls — iPhone

Touch to move; your ship fires automatically. Tap the pause button to pause, and the bolt to unleash the Vyric Surge once it’s charged.

Controls — Mac

Mouse or keyboard to move. P/Esc pauses, S fires the Vyric Surge. Keys can be remapped in Settings.

Saving & iCloud

Save from the pause menu. Signed in to iCloud, your saves follow you between iPhone and Mac — save on one, load on the other. Saves are encrypted: progress is earned, not edited.

Game Center

Sign in to appear on the leaderboard and earn the 49 achievements. Skip it and everything else works exactly the same.

Performance

iPhone: Settings → Frame Rate offers 30 / 60 / AUTO. 30 runs coolest; AUTO balances smoothness against temperature on long sessions.
Mac: Settings → Resolution sets the window size; Display settings control detail level.

Frequently asked

My saves don’t appear on my other device.

Check both devices use the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, then relaunch — sync usually completes within a minute. Still stuck? Email us.

Is there anything to buy?

No. One purchase, no in-app purchases, no ads, no accounts.

Where did my progress go?

Progress is per save slot — load your latest from LOAD GAME.

I found a bug.

Please email VyronAdmin@proton.me with your device and what happened. Bug reports genuinely shape what gets fixed first.
