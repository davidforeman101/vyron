First Flight

Everything here is learnable in a single run. Position, timing and target priority are the whole game — stay moving, keep the swarm from boxing you in, and read what each enemy is about to do before it does it.

The rest of this manual is what the ship's database would tell you, if you asked it nicely.

The Armory

Five weapon systems, each with a distinct tactical role. The right tool matters far more than the biggest one — and you can switch mid-battle, so switch.

Basic Cannon

100% Standard plasma projectiles. The yardstick every other weapon is measured against, and never the wrong answer.

Multi-Fire

120% Parallel bullets across a wider front. Trades precision for coverage — ideal when the screen fills up.

Rapid Laser

130% An instant beam with no travel time, so fast movers can't outrun it. It builds heat — ease off before it locks you out.

Chain Lightning

80% Arcs from one target to the next. Weakest on paper, devastating against a cluster — let them group up, then let go.

Smart Missile

200% Heavy ordnance with splash damage. Your answer to armour, and to anything that refuses to die on schedule.

Upgrades

Credits earned on every run feed the next unlock. There is always another gun to try and another upgrade to chase:

  • Weapon power — raw damage per shot
  • Guns & side guns — more barrels, wider coverage
  • Fire rate — shorter gaps between volleys
  • Shields & health — survive the mistakes
  • Drones — autonomous escorts that fight alongside you
  • Defence lasers — intercept what gets close

The Vyric Surge

Your ultimate. It charges as you fight and holds until you call on it — then hunts the screen clean and buys you the moment you needed.

Charging
Every kill fills the bar. Roughly fifty of them to fill it.
Release
Fire it when the bar is full — it launches and seeks the nearest threat.
Duration
Fifteen seconds of open season.
Behaviour
Homes target to target and bounces off the screen edges.
The catch
Kills made during a surge don't charge the next one. Charge only builds between them — so spend it when it counts, not when it's convenient.

Charge. Release. Dominate.

Kill Streaks

Destroy three of the same enemy type in a row and they'll drop a credit bonus. The tougher the enemy, the bigger the payout — so hunting one species deliberately pays better than clearing whatever's nearest.

Upgrade Tokens

Dropped at random once you're past the opening levels. Five tokens buy one free upgrade in the shop, and they keep between runs — so a bad run is never a wasted one.

Ship Evolution

Eight hulls, each with its own silhouette, colours and character — from the Vanguard you start in to the Colossus you earn. Credits from the campaign carry you up the ladder one hull at a time, and every step looks and feels like a promotion.

The Nemesis

One enemy remembers you. It returns across a campaign, and it is not the same ship each time — it studies how you fight and comes back built to beat it.

First meeting
Basic attacks. It breaks off at half health — and it isn't running.
The return
Shields tuned against the weapon you used last time.
The hunter
It predicts your movement, and it carries an escape pod.
Final reckoning
Full power, everything it has learned, and no way out for either of you.

It reads your weapon choice, your dodge patterns and the corners of the screen you retreat to. It wears the damage you did to it. Change how you fly, or it will have already worked out where you're going.

> It's watching.

The Swarm

Not one enemy but hundreds, flocking as a single organism around a core. You cannot shoot the middle of it until you've thinned the outside.

How to break it
Sustained fire strips its particles; the core is only exposed once enough of them are gone. Chain Lightning tears through the cluster, heavy missiles finish the core.
Variants
Some slow you with ice, some leave fire behind, some jam your weapons outright, some darken the screen and drag you in, and some poison the space they die in.
The gift
Every third swarm you destroy leaves a golden swarm behind that turns on its own kind and fights for you.

Combat Doctrine

  • Kill fast and keep killing — speed builds your score multiplier, and hesitation resets it.
  • Concentrated fire lands critical hits for double damage. Spraying does not.
  • Match the weapon to the threat. Switching mid-battle is free; dying is not.
  • Let enemies cluster before you reach for Chain Lightning.
  • Hold the Surge for the moment you're about to lose, not the moment you're winning.
  • Sovereigns freeze your progress. Kill them first, every time.
  • Against the Swarm, strip the outside before you go looking for the core.
  • Study the database before you fly. Everything out there has a weakness on file.
